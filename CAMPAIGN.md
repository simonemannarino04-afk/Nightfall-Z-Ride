# Boston: Apocalypse — Campaign

The campaign is structured as seven escalating missions across a fictionalized outbreak-era Boston. Locations are inspired by recognizable districts while layouts remain designed for gameplay rather than geographic simulation.

1. **Zero Hour — Back Bay**: outbreak tutorial, checkpoint power restoration and first defensive breach.
2. **The Green Line — Kenmore**: underground rescue mission culminating in an Alpha Runner encounter.
3. **Blackout — Beacon Hill**: night mission built around restoring the electrical grid and defending the substation.
4. **Last Shift — Downtown**: rescue and escort mission involving a stranded evacuation convoy and a Tank boss.
5. **Red Water — Boston Harbor**: investigation of the outbreak trail, research samples and infected nests.
6. **No Way Out — North End**: siege mission ending with a rooftop extraction attempt and Juggernaut encounter.
7. **Patient Zero — Quarantine Research Complex**: final infiltration, outbreak-data recovery, containment destruction and final boss.

Each mission is divided into objective checkpoints. Reaching an objective advances the checkpoint so the player can restart from meaningful campaign progress rather than replaying the entire mission. Weapon rewards escalate through the campaign from MP5 to the M82.
