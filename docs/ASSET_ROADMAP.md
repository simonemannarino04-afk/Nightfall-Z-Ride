# Nightfall Z Ride — Asset Roadmap

## Zombie roster
1. Civilian infected — baseline
2. Police infected — armored torso
3. Runner — fast, fragile
4. Construction worker — helmet protection
5. Brute — large silhouette, high health
6. Soldier infected — tougher late-wave enemy

## Vehicle
Survivor SUV/van with modeled dashboard, A/B pillars, seats, doors/windows, exterior hood, headlights and three passenger firing positions.

## Weapons
9mm pistol, SMG, assault rifle and pump shotgun. Each needs its own model, muzzle point, recoil, magazine size, damage and audio later.

## Map target
A ruined urban route built from reusable blocks: road intersections, apartment facades, shops, streetlights, wrecked vehicles, barricades, construction zone, tunnel, fuel station and extraction checkpoint.

## Mobile constraints
Prefer GLB assets, shared materials, texture atlases, LODs, instancing and compressed textures. Keep the browser prototype lightweight; production-quality assets should eventually move into a dedicated game-engine build.
